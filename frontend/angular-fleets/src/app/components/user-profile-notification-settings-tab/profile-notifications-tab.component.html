Notifications:

SCFleetFinder uses account links and emails for user verification and notification purposes only. This user information is secure, private, and will never be shared with third parties or other users without your explicit consent. Notifications are customizable so that you can receive only the notifications you are interested in. You can disable notifications or remove linked accounts and push notification configurations at any time.

Discord Notification Settings Link your Discord to have FleetyBot send your notifications as Discord messages.
  • Discord link enables you to log in to FleetFinder using your Discord account
  • You can also sign up for notifications through Discord — which are sent as direct messages from the FleetFinder Discord bot, FleetyBot.
  • Notifications are divided into three categories, which you can enable/disable individually.

Push Notification Settings Allow FleetFinder to send notifications to devices of your choosing — without needing to be logged in
  • Configure notifications for your devices and customize which types of notifications are sent to each device.
  • FleetFinder stores a unique token for each browser or device you enable. This token identifies where to deliver your notifications but contains no personal or account information — it cannot be used to identify you.
  • You can manage or remove any device's notification settings at any time.
