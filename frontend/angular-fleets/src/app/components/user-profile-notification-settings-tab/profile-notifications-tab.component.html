Notifications:

SCFleetFinder uses account links and emails for user verification and notification purposes only. This user information is secure, private, and will never be shared with third parties or other users without your explicit consent. Notifications are customizable so that you can receive only the notifications you are interested in. You can disable notifications or remove linked accounts and push notification configurations at any time.

Discord Notification Settings Link your Discord to have FleetyBot send your notifications as Discord messages.

Discord link enables you to log in to FleetFinder using your Discord account and configure notification settings to receive your notifications as direct messages from the FleetFinder Discord bot, FleetyBot. Notifications are divided into three categories, which you can enable/disable for Discord individually.
