Welcome to FleetFinder

Need a better crew?

 FleetFinder is a fan-operated companion tool for the Star Citizen MMO, helping players connect and form groups based on shared interests.

Hi all!

Thanks for checking out FleetFinder.

Things are still getting off the ground, so activity is light right now, but it works best when people post listings and check back in. If you're not sure exactly when you'll play, that's totally fine, just make a listing with a future date you might play on, and add something like "usually on weekends" or "weekdays after 6pm CST" in the description.

I think I went a little heavy on the create-listing form. That's getting simplified, and Discord login is coming soon to reduce friction on that front as well.

If you've ever thought, "I want to do more in Star Citizen, but I don't have the crew for it," that's exactly what this is for. No big events needed, just post what you want to do and see who bites.
