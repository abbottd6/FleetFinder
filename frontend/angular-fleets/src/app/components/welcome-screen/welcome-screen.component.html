Welcome to FleetFinder

Need a better crew?

If you've ever thought, "I want to do more in Star Citizen, but I can't find people to do it with," that's exactly what FleetFinder is for. Solo player who wants to try contested zones with a group? Running cargo and need an escort? Org looking for another org to have a fleet battle with? This is what FleetFinder is for.

If you don't see a listing that matches the gameplay you are looking for, create a listing and then check back. If you're not sure exactly when you'll play or a specific thing you are going to do, make a listing with a future date you might play on and a general description about what gameplay you are interested in.

I'm always looking for ways to improve the platform and/or make it easier to engage with. For feedback, suggestions, or reporting issues, you can email me at scfleetfinder@gmail.com, or respond to one of my (doctortalos) listings here with a message.
