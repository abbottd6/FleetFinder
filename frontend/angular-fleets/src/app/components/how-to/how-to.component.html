Create a Group
  • Create an account and verify your email (check your spam folder!)
  • Use the Create listing page and fill in the form fields with information about your group
  • Create the listing and wait for other users to message you
  • Once someone messages you, exchange in game credentials or comms credentials
  • Meet up at the Star Citizen login page
  • Join a group with your new crew members before you log in so you can be sure you are on the same server
  • You can delete or update your listings by visiting the Profile page and selecting your listing in the My Listings tab
  • There is currently a limit of 5 listings per user to reduce spam content
  • Listings currently have the following visibility classifications that are dependent on the amount of time that has passed since they were last updated:
    • Fresh (<3 hours)
    • Recent (<6 hours)
    • Stale (<12 hours)
    • Inactive (12+ hours)
    • These color codes are just quick visual cues to help people browsing
  • Listings expire after three days since their last update and no longer appear in other users search results
  • Listings that have not been updated for 14 days will be archived. Archived listings currently not recoverable, so it is advised that you save a template of the listing before it expires if you would like to reuse the listing later

Join a Group
  • Create an account and verify your email (check your spam folder!)
  • Use the Search listings page to search and filter for listings in your desired area of gameplay
  • Once you find a group that interests you, use the quick access menu (right click on desktop, long press on mobile) to message the user. You can also click (tap on mobile) the listing to view more details about the listing.
  • If the group owner invites you to join, exchange in game credentials or comms credentials
  • Meet up at the Star Citizen login page
  • Join a group with your new crew members before you log in so you can be sure you are on the same server

Reporting and Hiding Listings
  • Create an account and verify your email (check your spam folder!)
  • Hiding listings can be undone, reporting listings cannot
  • Both 'Hide' and 'Report' will exclude the selected listing from your search results.
  • Use the Quick Access Menu (desktop: right click, mobile: long press) to select either 'Hide' or 'Report'. You can also access these options from the detailed view of the listing through the 'Options' button
  • If you are reporting a listing, select a basis for the report
  • Hidden listings can be 'unhidden' using the 'Hidden' dropdown menu (to the right, above the search results) on the search page

Templates
  • Create an account and verify your email (check your spam folder!)
  • Create a listing with the details that you would like to save in a template
  • Navigate to the Profile page and ensure you are viewing the My Listings tab
  • Select a listing with the checkbox (only one listing can be templated at a time) or use the quick access menu (right click on the listing on desktop, or long press on mobile)
  • If using the checkbox, click the save template button
  • If using quick access, click template
  • You can view the template in the Profile -> Templates tab
  • To create a listing from a template, select the listing in the Templates tab, and use either the quick access menu or the Create from button
